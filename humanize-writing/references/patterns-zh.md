# Chinese AI-shaped Patterns

## How to use this catalog

These are contextual editing cues, not a banned-word list. Do not rewrite from one signal alone. Look for a cluster: repeated framing, unsupported evaluation, predictable syntax, and a mismatch with the document's purpose. A phrase-level match can be a false positive when it is accurate, required by genre, quoted, or part of an established domain term.

Judge each passage against nearby sentences. Keep language that carries facts, logic, stance, cadence, or necessary formality. The short examples below are illustrative, not replacement templates.

## Pattern catalog

### Chatbot artifacts

**Signals**
Answer-routing language such as `下面分三点说明`, `让我们先来看`, or `希望以上内容有所帮助`, especially when no live dialogue or navigation is needed. Also watch for a heading immediately restated as a conversational prompt.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The prose talks about delivering an answer instead of delivering the content. Several meta sentences around a short factual passage create a service-chat frame that the document never asked for.

**Keep when**
Keep genuine interaction cues in tutorials, workshops, support replies, or transcripts where the reader is actually being guided. One `请看下表` before a necessary table is not a problem.

**Rewrite move**
Remove the route announcement only when it carries no proposition, then retain the supplied fact: `下面我们来看看故障原因：连接池耗尽导致了故障。` → `连接池耗尽导致了故障。` Keep navigation that helps readers find material.

### Generic openings and closings

**Signals**
Broad era-setting openings (`在快速变化的环境中`) and summary closings (`总的来说`, `展望未来`) that add no scope, decision, or consequence. The stronger signal is a generic opener and generic closer enclosing otherwise specific facts.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The passage starts before its subject and ends after its evidence. This symmetrical wrapper makes unrelated documents share the same silhouette.

**Keep when**
Keep an opening that defines a real time period or operating condition, and a conclusion that makes a supported decision. Formal reports may require a labeled summary, but its content still needs to be specific.

**Rewrite move**
Remove only a zero-proposition wrapper. If an opening or closing carries evaluation, rewrite its shell while preserving that stance unless the user explicitly authorizes changing it.

### Inflated significance

**Signals**
Milestone, transformation, or destiny language clustered around routine events: `里程碑式进展`, `迈入全新阶段`, `具有划时代意义`, `开启崭新篇章`, especially without evidence of scale or lasting effect.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Evaluation outruns the facts. Repeated superlatives tell the reader how important an event is while withholding the criteria that would support the judgment.

**Keep when**
Keep significance claims when the text names the threshold, comparison, or consequence that earns them. A legally defined milestone or a quoted stakeholder assessment may also need exact preservation.

**Rewrite move**
Preserve both event and evaluation unless the user explicitly authorizes toning the evaluation down; revise only the shell: `旧接口停止接收新请求，这不仅是一次调整，更是一次关键跨越。` → `旧接口停止接收新请求；这既是一次调整，也是一次关键跨越。`

### Promotional language and vague attribution

**Signals**
Promotional stacks (`卓越体验`, `强大能力`, `全方位赋能`) or floating authorities (`业内普遍认为`, `有研究表明`, `用户一致认可`) with no named source, measure, or observable behavior.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The language borrows confidence from unnamed people and abstract praise. Several such phrases can turn neutral documentation into unsupported marketing copy.

**Keep when**
Keep a supplied, attributable quotation; a cited survey result; regulated product wording; or a brand term the user requires. Do not flatten intentionally promotional copy into technical prose unless requested.

**Rewrite move**
Preserve the supplied promotional stance while placing existing detail next to it: `操作体验更顺滑：列表首次加载从 900 ms 降到 600 ms。` → `列表首次加载从 900 ms 降到 600 ms，操作体验更顺滑。` Delete or tone down praise only with explicit authorization.

### Binary framing and false concessions

**Signals**
Repeated shells such as `这不仅是……更是……`, `不是……而是……`, or `虽然……但是……` when the first half is a straw position, both halves mean nearly the same thing, or the contrast is unsupported.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Manufactured opposition gives ordinary statements a dramatic turn. Multiple concession shells also hide which proposition is actually being asserted.

**Keep when**
Keep real negation, correction, exception, and contrast. In `这不是回归问题，而是配置错误`, both polarity and diagnosis may be essential semantic anchors.

**Rewrite move**
State the supplied propositions directly while preserving polarity and evaluation: `处理时间缩短了，这不仅提升效率，更体现团队决心。` → `处理时间缩短了，既提升了效率，也体现了团队决心。` Do not collapse a real condition or exception.

### Mechanical sequence and rule of three

**Signals**
Forced `首先/其次/最后`, `一是/二是/三是`, or exactly three parallel benefits when the ideas are unequal, inseparable, or not sequential. Repeated paragraph openings strengthen the signal.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The count organizes surface form rather than thought. It can imply order, completeness, or equal weight that the source does not establish.

**Keep when**
Keep numbered procedures, ranked findings, contractual elements, and genuinely distinct lists. Three items are not inherently artificial; the problem is an imposed structure.

**Rewrite move**
Use causal or topical grouping, merge dependent points, or remove numbering. Preserve sequence words when changing the order would change the instructions.

### Symmetry and parallel shells

**Signals**
Several sentences with identical length and grammar, paired slogans (`既要……也要……`), or clauses balanced mainly for cadence. Watch for each paragraph ending in the same evaluative noun phrase.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Excessive balance makes independently weighted ideas sound interchangeable. The polished rhythm becomes more visible than the content.

**Keep when**
Keep deliberate rhetoric, poetry, speeches, mnemonic instructions, and parallel legal provisions. Also keep naturally recurring syntax when it improves comparison.

**Rewrite move**
Let importance determine sentence length. Combine tightly related clauses, give the main claim a direct sentence, and retain only the parallelism that clarifies a real comparison.

### Unsupported emotion, opinion, and intimacy

**Signals**
材料未支撑的情绪、观点或亲密感，例如 `令人振奋的是`, `我们都知道`, `相信你也会`, or a personal reaction that neither the target nor its facts establish.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The prose assigns a feeling, stance, or relationship to the writer or reader instead of earning it from the supplied content. In clusters, these cues can replace observable facts with assumed rapport.

**Keep when**
Keep emotion, opinion, and familiarity stated in the target as semantic anchors. A sample may establish register, but never supplies a new feeling or stance for the target.

**Rewrite move**
Rephrase the cue while retaining the source's emotion, opinion, or familiarity. Remove or tone it down only with explicit authorization; if the stance cannot be preserved naturally, keep it or ask.

### Fake engagement

**Signals**
Unanswered questions (`你是否想过……`), assumed reader feelings (`相信你也能感受到`), commands to imagine, or inclusive `我们` where no shared identity has been established.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The text simulates rapport without evidence about the reader. A cluster of questions and assurances can feel like scripted engagement rather than an authored stance.

**Keep when**
Keep questions that the next sentence genuinely answers, established community language, interview copy, classroom prompts, and a user's consistent first-person plural voice.

**Rewrite move**
Turn the supplied proposition into a direct claim without adding a metric: `你有没有发现页面加载更快了？` → `页面加载更快了。` Keep a genuine question when it performs a real function.

### Nominalization, passive voice, and translation tone

**Signals**
Dense chains like `对流程进行优化与能力建设`, missing actors, repeated `被/得以/予以`, long attributive phrases, or literal connective patterns that sound translated. Diagnose a cluster, not any single passive sentence.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Actions disappear into abstract nouns and accountability becomes vague. Translation-shaped syntax may also force Chinese information into an unnatural order.

**Keep when**
Keep passive voice when the actor is unknown, sensitive, obvious, or intentionally backgrounded; keep established technical and policy nominalizations; preserve high-risk wording whose scope depends on form.

**Rewrite move**
Restore a supplied subject and concrete verb, split overloaded modifiers, and order information by Chinese discourse logic: `值班同学完成了对配置的更新` → `值班同学更新了配置`.

### Formatting

**Signals**
Every sentence turned into a bullet, repeated bold lead-ins, headings that merely echo the first line, excessive colons, decorative separators, or one-sentence paragraphs created without rhetorical purpose.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Formatting manufactures hierarchy that the content does not have. Uniform blocks can make a short explanation resemble a generated outline.

**Keep when**
Keep user-supplied Markdown, required templates, checklists, scan-friendly procedures, and accessibility structure. Never alter URLs or Markdown link targets while simplifying presentation.

**Rewrite move**
Preserve the document's format by default. Merge only artificial fragments, remove redundant labels, and use bullets when the items are genuinely parallel or actionable.

### Lexical clusters

**Signals**
Nearby abstract buzzwords such as `赋能`, `闭环`, `生态`, `焕新`, `共创`, `持续发力`, and `高质量发展`, especially when several modify one unsupported outcome. Repetition across paragraph endings is more diagnostic than one occurrence.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Stacked abstractions create the impression of substance without identifying actors, actions, or results. Swapping them for a different buzzword stack does not solve the problem.

**Keep when**
Keep defined organizational terms, quoted language, proper names, and words whose concrete referent is clear in context. `闭环` may be precise if the text actually names detection, response, and verification steps.

**Rewrite move**
Name the actor, action, object, or measured result already present. Do not delete an evaluative lexical layer without explicit authorization, mechanically substitute a synonym, or edit solely because a listed word appears.

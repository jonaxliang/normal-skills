# English AI-shaped Patterns

## How to use this catalog

These are contextual editing cues, not a banned-word list. Do not rewrite from one signal alone. Look for a cluster: repeated framing, unsupported evaluation, predictable syntax, and a mismatch with the document's purpose. A phrase-level match can be a false positive when it is accurate, genre-required, quoted, or an established term.

Read the surrounding paragraph before changing anything. Preserve language that carries evidence, logic, stance, cadence, or necessary formality. The examples are brief illustrations, not replacement templates.

## Pattern catalog

### Chatbot artifacts

**Signals**
Answer-management phrases such as `Let's break this down`, `Here's what you need to know`, and `I hope this helps`, especially when the text is not a conversation. A heading followed by a second sentence announcing the same topic is another cluster signal.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The prose narrates the act of answering instead of presenting the answer. Multiple routing phrases can make an article, memo, or product note sound like a support bot.

**Keep when**
Keep real navigation in tutorials, workshops, support replies, and transcripts. A concise pointer to a table or next step can be useful when it helps the reader move through the material.

**Rewrite move**
Delete the route announcement only when it carries no proposition, then retain the supplied fact: `Let's unpack the outage: it began when the connection pool reached its limit.` → `The outage began when the connection pool reached its limit.` Preserve navigation that performs a real function.

### Generic openings and closings

**Signals**
Scene-setting like `In today's rapidly changing landscape` and conclusions such as `Ultimately` or `In conclusion` that add no scope, decision, or consequence. A generic opening paired with a future-facing closing is a stronger signal than either phrase alone.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The text delays its subject, then restates importance after the evidence has ended. Unrelated documents acquire the same opening-body-summary silhouette.

**Keep when**
Keep context that defines a real period, constraint, or trend, and keep conclusions that synthesize supported findings or record a decision. Formal genres may require a conclusion heading.

**Rewrite move**
Remove only a zero-proposition wrapper. If an opening or closing carries evaluation, rewrite its shell while preserving that stance unless the user explicitly authorizes changing it.

### Inflated significance

**Signals**
Routine events described as `pivotal`, `transformative`, `groundbreaking`, `a testament`, `a major milestone`, or `a new era`, especially in clusters without a threshold, comparison, or durable consequence.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The evaluation is larger than its evidence. Repeated significance labels instruct the reader to admire an event instead of showing why it matters.

**Keep when**
Keep the assessment when the source provides criteria that earn it, when `milestone` has a defined project meaning, or when the wording is an attributable quotation.

**Rewrite move**
Preserve both event and evaluation unless the user explicitly authorizes toning the evaluation down; revise only the shell: `The legacy endpoint is no longer in the request path; this is not merely an adjustment but a pivotal leap.` → `The legacy endpoint is no longer in the request path; this is both an adjustment and a pivotal leap.`

### Promotional language and vague attribution

**Signals**
Stacks such as `seamless, robust, best-in-class experience` or floating authorities like `experts agree`, `research shows`, and `users love` without a named source, citation, measure, or observable behavior.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Abstract praise and unnamed consensus create borrowed certainty. In neutral prose, several such claims can sound like generic campaign copy.

**Keep when**
Keep supplied brand language, regulated product wording, attributable quotations, cited findings, and intentionally promotional register when that is the assignment.

**Rewrite move**
Preserve supplied promotional stance and place an existing property or attribution next to it. Delete or tone down praise only with explicit authorization; never fabricate a metric or customer example to make it more specific.

### Binary framing and false concessions

**Signals**
Repeated `not just X but Y`, `it's not X; it's Y`, or `while X, Y` shells where X is a straw position, both halves overlap, or the contrast lacks evidence.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Manufactured opposition gives an ordinary statement a dramatic pivot. Several concession shells also obscure which proposition carries the actual meaning.

**Keep when**
Keep real correction, negation, exception, and contrast. In `This is not a regression; it is a configuration error`, polarity may be an exact semantic anchor.

**Rewrite move**
State the supplied propositions directly while preserving polarity and evaluation: `The response time is shorter; it is not merely faster but redefines performance.` → `The response time is shorter and also redefines performance.`

### Mechanical sequence and rule of three

**Signals**
Forced `First, Second, Third`, exactly three benefits, or repeated numbered paragraphs when the ideas are unequal, dependent, or not sequential. Watch for filler added only to complete a trio.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The count organizes surface form rather than reasoning. It may imply order, completeness, or equal weight that the source never establishes.

**Keep when**
Keep procedures, ranked findings, contractual elements, and truly parallel lists. Three items are not inherently suspect; an imposed structure is.

**Rewrite move**
Group by cause or topic, merge dependent ideas, or remove numbering. Preserve sequence markers when changing the order would change an instruction or chronology.

### Symmetry and parallel shells

**Signals**
Several sentences with identical length and grammar, slogan pairs, balanced clauses built mainly for cadence, or every paragraph ending in the same evaluative phrase.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Excessive balance gives differently weighted ideas the same shape. The polish becomes more prominent than the point.

**Keep when**
Keep deliberate rhetoric, speeches, poetry, mnemonic instructions, legal parallelism, and syntax that supports a real comparison.

**Rewrite move**
Let importance determine sentence length. Combine dependent clauses, give the main claim a direct sentence, and retain only symmetry that clarifies the relationship.

### Unsupported emotion, opinion, and intimacy

**Signals**
Signals of unsupported emotion, opinion, or intimacy include `what surprised me most`, `you'll love`, or `we all know`, especially when the target supplies no personal reaction, shared belief, or relationship with the reader.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The prose assigns a feeling, stance, or familiarity to the writer or reader instead of earning it from the supplied content. A cluster can replace observable facts with assumed rapport.

**Keep when**
Keep emotion, opinion, and familiarity stated in the target as semantic anchors. A sample may establish register, but never supplies a new feeling or stance for the target.

**Rewrite move**
Rephrase the cue while retaining the source's emotion, opinion, or familiarity. Remove or tone it down only with explicit authorization; if the stance cannot be preserved naturally, keep it or ask.

### Fake engagement

**Signals**
Rhetorical questions with obvious answers, `Imagine a world where`, assumed reactions (`You can probably feel the difference`), or inclusive `we` without an established shared group.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
The text simulates rapport without evidence about the reader. A cluster of prompts and reassurances can feel scripted rather than personally voiced.

**Keep when**
Keep questions the next sentence genuinely investigates, classroom prompts, interviews, established community language, and a consistent first-person voice supplied by the writer.

**Rewrite move**
Turn the supplied proposition into a direct claim without adding a metric: `Have you noticed that the page now loads faster?` → `The page now loads faster.` Keep a genuine question when it performs a real function.

### Nominalization, passive voice, and translation tone

**Signals**
Dense noun chains (`the implementation of an optimization initiative`), missing actors, repeated passives, stacked prepositional phrases, or connectors and word order that closely mirror another language. Diagnose the cluster, not one passive clause.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Actions disappear into abstractions and responsibility becomes vague. Translation-shaped syntax can also put background information where English expects the subject or action.

**Keep when**
Keep passive voice when the actor is unknown, sensitive, obvious, or intentionally backgrounded. Preserve established domain nominalizations and high-stakes wording whose scope depends on convention.

**Rewrite move**
Restore a supplied actor and concrete verb, split overloaded modifiers, and reorder information for English emphasis: `The operator performed an update of the configuration` → `The operator updated the configuration`.

### Formatting

**Signals**
Every sentence presented as a bullet, repeated bold lead-ins, headings echoed by the first line, excessive colons, decorative separators, or arbitrary one-sentence paragraphs.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Formatting manufactures hierarchy the content does not contain. Uniform blocks can turn a short explanation into a generic generated outline.

**Keep when**
Keep supplied Markdown, required templates, checklists, accessible structure, and scan-friendly procedures. Preserve URLs and Markdown link targets exactly.

**Rewrite move**
Preserve format by default. Merge only artificial fragments, remove redundant labels, and reserve bullets for genuinely parallel or actionable items.

### Lexical clusters

**Signals**
Nearby abstractions such as `leverage`, `unlock`, `empower`, `ecosystem`, `holistic`, `elevate`, `foster`, and `drive impact`, especially when several point to one unsupported outcome. Repetition across paragraph endings is stronger evidence than one word.

**Why it reads as AI-shaped**
Stacked abstractions imply substance without naming actors, actions, or results. Replacing them with a different buzzword cluster leaves the problem intact.

**Keep when**
Keep defined organizational terms, quotations, proper names, and words with a concrete referent in context. `Ecosystem` may be precise when the passage names its participants and relationships.

**Rewrite move**
Use the actor, action, object, or measured result already in the source. Do not delete an evaluative lexical layer without explicit authorization, and never edit solely because one listed word appears.
